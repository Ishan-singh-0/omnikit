What it does

Every warning is a plain rule with a source. Nothing is a black box.

Fall and SOS on the device

Impact then stillness, or a 3-second button hold. Decided on the ESP32, so it works with no network.

Climb vs storm

GPS altitude corrects the barometer, so only real weather counts as a storm.

Explainable risk score

0 to 100 from named rules: pressure, heat, cold, route, stillness, silence, air.

Dead-man’s switch

No packet for 5 or 15 minutes raises a flag and keeps the last known position.

Rescue brief

Who, where, why and a map link, sent to a webhook or Telegram on danger.

Honest timestamps

Packets queued in a dead zone carry their age, so the history is true.

What it is not

  • A prototype and decision aid, not a diagnosis or a replacement for a map, compass or emergency beacon.
  • Fall thresholds are not yet validated outdoors; a dropped unit looks like a fall.
  • GPS needs open sky. The SIM800L is 2G only and is not satellite.
  • MQ-135 shows that the air changed, never which gas.
